## About Me

I am a PhD candidate in Mathematics and Statistics at Sorbonne University in Paris.
My main research interests are statistical learning, stochastic optimization, and optimal transport.
I am also a former Student Researcher at Google Research, where I worked on the Operations Research team, building stable routes for vehicle routing problems under distribution perturbations.


My PhD is supervised by [A. Godichon-Baggioni](https://godichon.perso.math.cnrs.fr/),
[F-X. Vialard](https://angkor.univ-mlv.fr/~vialard/) and
[O. Wintenberger](https://wintenberger.fr).

**Languages**: French (Native), English (C1, TOEIC), Russian (B2)

About Me

I am a PhD candidate in Mathematics and Statistics at Sorbonne University in Paris. My main research interests are statistical learning, stochastic optimization, and optimal transport. I am also a former Student Researcher at Google Research, where I worked on the Operations Research team, building stable routes for vehicle routing problems under distribution perturbations.

My PhD is supervised by A. Godichon-Baggioni, F-X. Vialard and O. Wintenberger.

Languages: French (Native), English (C1, TOEIC), Russian (B2)
